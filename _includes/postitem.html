{%- comment -%} One row in a post list. Used by the homepage, /blog/ and the topic pages, so the three cannot drift apart. The thumbnail is the post's own image where it has one, and otherwise a generated placeholder. That placeholder is DETERMINISTIC: its angle and stop position come from the post's own publication epoch, so a given post draws the same figure on every build and every page load, and two posts do not collide. Its three colours are design tokens — all near-neutral in both themes — so the set reads as one family and follows light/dark for free rather than being a hardcoded grey.
