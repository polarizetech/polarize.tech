This is a placeholder. It exists so the machinery has something to chew on — but
it is written under the same rules every real post will be, because a protocol
you exempt the first post from is not a protocol.

## The rules this page obeys

Nothing here is cited from memory. Every source is referenced by a **ledger key**,
and that key resolves to an entry in a machine-generated citation ledger that was
built by querying Crossref and PubMed directly. No author name, year, or DOI on
this page was typed by a human or a model — they are copied out of the registry
response. That is the whole point: an error then gets fixed in one place instead
of eleven.

The claim below is tiered `[C]` — our own inference, stated as such. Bioelectric
state is argued to be an *instructive* control layer for morphogenesis rather than
a downstream readout of genetic signaling{% include cite.html key="levin2018" %},
building on the earlier account of endogenous voltage potentials controlling cell
behaviour in vivo{% include cite.html key="levin2014" %}. Neither of those sources
has been read in full text yet, which is recorded rather than glossed over — and
which is exactly why this sits at `[C]` and not `[A]`.

## What "could be wrong" means

Every claim published here is maintained in a separate research repository with
recorded **disproof conditions** and a section for **disconfirming evidence**.
A confidence tier is computed from those records; it is never asserted by whoever
happens to be writing. If a source is retracted, or a disproof condition is met,
the claim is amended in writing under a dated retraction notice — never quietly
deleted.
