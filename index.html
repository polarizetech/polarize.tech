01 — The Concept

A battery in every cell.

A living cell is wrapped in a fatty membrane just two molecules thick. That membrane is a wall — and a gatekeeper. It carefully sorts electrically charged particles called ions, keeping some out and locking others in.

The result is a difference in charge between the inside and outside of the cell: the membrane potential. The cell is polarized. It is, quite literally, charged — holding roughly −70 millivolts at rest, the same order of magnitude as a bolt of lightning packed into a space a million times thinner than a human hair.

  • −70mV Resting potential of a typical neuron
  • 5nm Thickness of the cell membrane
  • 100m/s Speed of a nerve impulse

03 — The Signal Travels

One spark becomes a message.

A single depolarization doesn't stay put. It triggers its neighbour, which triggers the next — a self-renewing wave racing down the length of a nerve fibre without ever losing strength.

This is how a thought leaves your brain and becomes a movement in your hand. Not a flow of electrons through a wire, but a relay of charge leaping from gate to gate through living tissue.

“The body electric is not a metaphor. It is the literal medium of life's fastest conversations.”
04 — Why It Matters

Voltage shapes life itself.

The Thinking Mind

Eighty-six billion neurons polarize and fire in concert — the electrical symphony we experience as thought, memory, and consciousness.

The Beating Heart

A wave of depolarization sweeps the heart 100,000 times a day, each pulse a perfectly timed bioelectric command to contract.

The Healing Body

Wounds generate their own electric fields. Cells read this voltage like a map, navigating toward injury to rebuild what was lost.

The Blueprint of Form

Emerging science suggests bioelectric patterns help decide where a limb, an eye, or an organ should grow — a code written in charge.
