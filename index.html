Process

Built to show where we were wrong.

Every study borrows the discipline of a clinical trial: the inputs and the predictions are preregistered before anything runs, so a result can fail in public and stay on the record. These three repositories are how that works.

A prediction that fails is reported as a fail. Mostly negative results are still results.

Every change to a plan is dated

No claim without its source

Every claim cites a verbatim passage; claims that fail a check are removed and counted.

Design system

polarize-uiTypeScript + zero-build CSSMIT

Science is beautiful. Its web presence should be too.

The design system behind these pages, built for interfaces that show scientific data honestly. Every measured, modelled or speculative value carries a label, and the label can never be hidden.

Charts
A threshold is drawn on the axes it judges; a log axis says so in its label.
Signals
Heatmap and Waveform on a one-hue ramp, with clipping counted, not hidden.
Statistics
An observed statistic against its null, the permutation p printed with its count.
Evidence
Tier, Value and Readout: measured and predicted are never styled alike.
